Your field guide

Move: WASD / arrows, or the touch joystick. Tap a creature to select it; Tab or Target cycles nearby foes.

Combat: Your basic attack is automatic in range. Open Skills to spend training points. Abilities unlock at levels 1, 1, 4, 7 and 10. Use 1–5 or the hotbar for spells. Each class has a unique resource and signature ability: press F or tap its button, unlocked at level 3. Each skill has its own mana cost and cooldown; area spells land on your selected enemy. Tap or focus a skill to read its description below the bar.

Travel & tactics: R toggles trail running outside combat. G uses your racial ability. Moving interrupts longer spell casts. Discover camps on foot to unlock return travel from other camps. Pick an unexplored region on the world map to follow a blue road marker.

Survive: Space / Dodge avoids damage briefly. Q / Tonic heals. Orange circles and lines are incoming attacks—move away or interrupt them. Purple enemies cast at range.

Explore: Collect glowing gear drops, then open Gear to compare and equip items. Export a backup from Saves before switching devices. Return to the lantern camp to heal, upgrade gear, change class or appearance and read your story journal. Follow gold markers through 211 quests across 25 connected regions. Visit local quest givers to accept quests and turn them in for rewards. Carry up to eight quests and select one in the Journal to follow its arrow. Press E near a marker to interact. Creatures respawn after 30 seconds; the guardian takes 90 seconds.

Escape pauses. Opening menus pauses combat too. This is single-player, not an online multiplayer server. Progress autosaves on this browser. Use Saves & backups to move your adventure to another device.
